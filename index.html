Weld Heat Input Check

Support for the iPhone and iPad app

Weld Heat Input Check is a field calculator and pass log for welders, welding inspectors and QC technicians who need to confirm heat input against a qualified WPS or PQR. It is made by Jeffrey Fletcher, who works in welding quality control.

Common questions

Which formula does it use?
Heat input = volts × amps × 60 ÷ travel speed, per ASME IX QW-409.1 and AWS D1.1, with no arc efficiency factor. Arc energy (J) and Power (W) methods are available for waveform-controlled power sources. ISO/EN 1011-1 applies a process efficiency factor and will read lower.

How do I get travel speed from a timed pass?
Turn on "Travel speed from length & time", enter the weld length, then use the arc timer: Start when the arc strikes, Stop when it breaks.

How accurate is the camera measurement?
It is a convenience for longer welds and works best on iPhone Pro models with LiDAR. For short coupons, a tape is more accurate.

Where is my log stored, and how do I back it up?
Only on your device. Use Export CSV in the Pass log to save a copy to Files, Mail or AirDrop.

Does it need a signal?
No. The app works fully offline.
