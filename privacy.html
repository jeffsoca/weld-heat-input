Privacy Policy

Weld Heat Input Check for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 25 November 2026

Weld Heat Input Check is a calculator and log for welding heat input. It is designed so that your data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it.

What the app stores

  • The weld readings, WPS limits and settings you enter, so they are still there when you reopen the app.
  • The passes you save to the log: joint ID, pass number, welder name or stamp, WPS number, notes, readings, date and time.

All of this is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage. It is included in your normal iCloud or computer backup of the device if you have backups turned on.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.
  • It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • It does not require an account and does not connect to the internet.

Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to measure a weld length. Frames are processed live on the device for that measurement. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Exporting

When you tap Export CSV or share a pass, the app hands the file or text to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there (Mail, Files, AirDrop, another app) is under your control and governed by that service's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete a pass from the Pass log, or delete all passes from the log's menu. Deleting the app removes all of its data.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
